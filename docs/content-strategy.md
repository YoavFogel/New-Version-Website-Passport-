# Content Strategy: EU Passport (new site)

**Date:** 2026-10-07
**Language of the site:** Hebrew (RTL). This strategy document is in English; all site copy is in `/docs/content/`.

---

## 1. Business goals

1. **Generate qualified leads** for Romanian citizenship cases, meaning people who are likely eligible and understand the language requirement before the first call.
2. **Cut time spent on unqualified calls** by answering the main questions on the site: Am I eligible? Do I need Romanian? What about my kids? How long does it take? What does it cost?
3. **Build trust** as an attorney-led, family-founded office that has been through the process itself.
4. **Rank organically** for the main Hebrew search terms around Romanian citizenship and the Romanian passport.

## 2. Audiences

| Persona | Situation | Main question | What they need from us |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The researcher** (30–55) | A parent or grandparent was born in Romania, Bessarabia or Bukovina | "Am I eligible, and is it worth it?" | A clear eligibility explanation and a quick self-check |
| **The parent** | Wants an EU passport mainly for their children | "Will my kids get it? Do they need Romanian?" | Minors page, family pricing logic |
| **The worried applicant** | Heard about "the new language law" | "Is it still possible without fluent Romanian?" | An honest B1 explanation, exemptions, how we help |
| **The senior** (65+) | Born in Romania, or a child of someone born there | "Am I exempt from the language test? Can I restore my citizenship?" | Restoration page, exemption clarity, help with paperwork |
| **The returning lead** | Started the process years ago or with another provider | "Where does my file stand? Can you take over?" | Process page, contact |
| **The citizen** | Already holds Romanian citizenship | "How do I get or renew the passport?" | Passport page |

## 4. Voice and tone

**Voice:** an experienced attorney explaining a complicated process to a family member. Calm, precise, warm, never pushy.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "בדרך כלל", "ברוב המקרים", "לפי הניסיון שלנו" | "מובטח", "100%", "בקלי קלות", "הכי מהיר" |
| Explain *why* a document is needed | List documents without context |
| Give ranges with a date ("נכון לאוקטובר 2026") | Give a single number with no date |
| Use "אתם" (plural, inclusive) consistently | Mix "אתה/את/אתם" |
| Short paragraphs (2–4 lines on mobile) | Walls of text |
| Name the authority (הרשות הלאומית לאזרחות, ANC) | Vague "הרומנים", "המשרד שם" |
| "נבדוק", "נלווה", "נסביר" | "נדאג להכול", "אתם לא צריכים לעשות כלום" |
| Admit limits ("ההחלטה בידי הרשויות ברומניה") | Imply that we control the outcome |
| Use the hyphen or comma in Hebrew text | Use the em dash (house style) |

**Terminology (standardize):**
- Brand: **EU Passport** (Latin letters) in logos and titles; in running Hebrew text use "EU Passport" and not "אי.יו פספורט". **[decide]**
- "אזרחות רומנית" is the main term. "דרכון רומני" is the product at the end of the process. Don't use them as synonyms.
- "השבת אזרחות" = restoration under Article 11. Use "סעיף 11" in parentheses once per page for precision.
- "הרשות הלאומית לאזרחות ברומניה (ANC)" on first mention, then "הרשות".
- "תעודת B1 בשפה הרומנית".
- "קונסוליה" for the consulate. Use "שגרירות" only when it is literally the embassy.

## 5. Fact-labeling system

The site must clearly separate three kinds of statements. Each page uses three visual callout types (to be designed later):

| Label (Hebrew) | Use for | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **⚖️ מה קובע החוק** | Statements that come from the law or official rules. They must cite the source (law / article / date) and have a "last reviewed" date. | "מאז 14 במרץ 2025 נדרשת תעודת B1 בשפה הרומנית ממבקשי השבת אזרחות, למעט החריגים שבחוק." |
| **⏱️ הערכת זמנים** | Time estimates from our experience. Always a range, always dated, always says it can change. | "לפי הניסיון שלנו בשנה האחרונה, הטיפול בבקשה ברומניה נמשך בדרך כלל X–Y חודשים." |
| **💡 הסבר כללי** | Plain-language explanations, tips and context. | "ככל שיש בידיכם יותר מסמכים של ההורה או הסב שנולדו ברומניה, השלב הראשון מתקצר." |

Every content page also ends with:

## 6. Conversion strategy

**Primary conversion:** the **eligibility questionnaire** (`/bdikat-zakaut`). It is short (6–8 questions), works well on mobile, and ends with a booking request. It qualifies leads and collects the data the office needs for the first call.

**Secondary conversions:**
- WhatsApp click (sticky button on mobile, with a prefilled message per page)
- Phone click (`tel:`)
- Contact form (short: name, phone, preferred contact time, free text)

**CTA hierarchy (same wording site-wide):**
1. Primary button: **"לבדיקת זכאות"** → questionnaire
2. Secondary: **"שיחה בוואטסאפ"**
3. Tertiary (text link): **"או התקשרו: 02-509-0307"** **[V22]**

**Placement:** CTA in the hero; after the section that answers the page's main question; and a closing CTA block. Never more than one primary CTA per screen on mobile.

**Trust elements near CTAs:** "שיחת היכרות ללא התחייבות" **[V21: confirm the first call is free]**, the founder's name and photo, and one short testimonial.

## 9. Content governance
- **Owner:** the responsible attorney approves every `⚖️` statement.
- **Review cycle:** legal pages every 6 months, and immediately after any change in Romanian law or ANC practice.
- **"Last updated" date** visible on every topic page.
- **Testimonials:** only real clients, with written consent, first name + last initial (or full name if agreed), city and year. No fabricated or "composite" testimonials.
- **Numbers** (families helped, years active): only with internal records to back them.

## 10. Mobile and RTL guidelines (for copy)
- Headings ≤ 6 words where possible. Paragraphs ≤ 4 lines on a 375px screen.
- Use numbered steps and accordions (FAQ, documents) instead of long lists.
- Mixed-direction text: put Latin terms (B1, ANC, CNP, EU Passport) on the same line with care. Don't start a Hebrew sentence with a Latin token.
- Phone numbers in LTR spans (`02-509-0307`).
- Dates in Hebrew format (14 במרץ 2025).
- Sticky bottom bar on mobile: [לבדיקת זכאות] [וואטסאפ].
