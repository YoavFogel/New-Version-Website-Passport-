**3. CTA:** none.
**4. H1:** מדיניות פרטיות
**5. Must cover:**
- What data is collected (forms, questionnaire, WhatsApp, cookies/analytics)
- Purpose: checking eligibility, contacting the person, managing the case
- Third parties: CRM (monday.com), messaging (WhatsApp), analytics, and Romanian partner attorneys (only for clients)
- Data retention
- Rights: access, correction, deletion
- Marketing consent (separate and optional, per the anti-spam law)
- Contact for privacy requests
